Privacy

Power Analytics website and Android app · last updated 26 October 2026

In short

Power Analytics shows charts and notes built from public power-market data (EPİAŞ, Elexon, NESO). You do not create an account, and neither the website nor the app asks for your name, e-mail, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Visit counting

The website counts page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It records which page was opened, the referring site, screen size and browser type. It does not set tracking cookies, does not store your IP address and cannot identify you. The app opens the same website, so the same counting applies there.

The Android app

The app is a window onto this website. It requests only internet access. It stores nothing about you; the only data on your phone is the normal web cache that lets pages load faster, which is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Links to other sites (LinkedIn, data sources, GitHub) open in their own app or your browser, where their own privacy rules apply.

Sharing and selling

No personal data is collected, so none is shared or sold.

Children

The service is meant for adults interested in energy markets and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this changes (for example if subscriptions are added), this page will say so before the change takes effect. Questions: Ali Bingül on LinkedIn.
